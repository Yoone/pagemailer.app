Logos & Socials

Logos & Socials settings are accessible as a tab on PageMailer’s settings page. You can find the app’s settings by following this step-by-step guide.

Social media links

You can optionally set up social media links that can be displayed in the footer of your email communications. This is an option that can be turned on or off on a per-template basis, and you can find out more about how to use it in the template settings.

Adding a social media link

  • Make sure you are on the Logos & Socials settings tab.
  • Select your social media platform from the list, which determines the icon shown in the email.
  • Enter your link, and click Add.

Logos

Scroll down on the Logos & Socials tab, and you will find the logos management section of the page.

You can upload up to 10 logos for your organization, to make sure that your branding is always consistent with the contents of your emails and their target audience. Logos are referenced by templates, so make sure to configure those next.

Renaming logos

To rename a logo (for your internal use, does not affect configured templates), click on its name, and a text field will appear. Simply update the name and click somewhere else on the page to save.
